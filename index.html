Stay Prepared
Safety Tips
01
Share your location

Always share your live location with a trusted person when traveling alone at night.

02
Stay aware of surroundings

Avoid wearing headphones in both ears and keep your phone accessible but not visible.

03
Trust your instincts

If something feels wrong, leave the situation immediately. Your gut feeling matters.

04
Have a code word

Set up a distress code word with family or friends that signals you need help urgently.

05
Keep phone charged

Carry a power bank. A dead phone in an emergency can be dangerous.
